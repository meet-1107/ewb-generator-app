How to delete your E-Way Easy account and its data

Option 1: From the app

  1. Open the E-Way Easy app and sign in.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Request account deletion and confirm.
  4. Your email app opens with the request filled in. Send it.

Tip: before deleting, use Account → Export my data to keep a copy of your invoices and e-way bills.

Option 2: By email (without the app)

Send an email to bvbtrade9@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request, from the email address you use to sign in to E-Way Easy.

We may reply to confirm the request comes from you.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (email or Google).
  • Your business profile, GSTINs, address, phone and bank details.
  • Invoices, buyers, products, transporters and vehicles saved in the app.
  • Your saved e-way bill portal login.
  • Your wallet balance. Unused credits are not refunded.

We delete this data within 30 days of confirming your request.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment records (amount, date, Razorpay payment ID): kept for up to 8 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting law.
  • Consent and security logs: kept for up to 1 year, to resolve disputes and prevent misuse.

E-way bills already generated stay on the government NIC e-way bill system; we cannot delete them there.
