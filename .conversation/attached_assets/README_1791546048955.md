# EWB Mobile App — Screen Specifications

Product specification for a simple Android/iOS app that helps Indian vendors create sales invoices, prepare e-way bills (EWB), track saved bills, and check GST return status.

## Product direction

- **Audience:** small through large vendors who do not use Mirable.
- **Primary job:** turn a sales invoice into a reviewed, valid e-way bill with as little re-entry as possible.
- **Platforms:** iOS and Android; proposed client is Expo / React Native with TypeScript.
- **Authentication:** sign-in is the first app screen. No business data is visible until authenticated.
- **Backend:** Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, and trusted Cloud Functions. Do not call GST/EWB provider APIs directly from the mobile app.
- **Language and units:** launch with English and INR; keep labels/localization and Indian number formatting ready for expansion.

## Navigation

Bottom navigation: **Dashboard · Invoices · E-way bills · GST status · Account**.

The Dashboard also exposes prominent **New invoice** and **Import PDF** actions. Invoice-to-EWB generation is a guided flow, not a separate dense form. Account contains subscription, business settings, help, and sign-out.

## Decisions and assumptions to verify

1. “5 to 6 predefined sales invoices” is interpreted as six reusable invoice templates/layouts. A vendor can choose one, save a customized version, and still edit each invoice's data. The templates must not imply government-approved formats.
2. “Verified EWB” means the provider/NIC reports a bill as valid/active (or another clearly defined verification result). Show the source and last-checked time; do not treat local data as government verification.
3. PDF extraction is a draft aid only. The user must review and confirm all parsed values before any submission.
4. GSTR-1, GSTR-2, and GSTR-3B status requires an authorized GST data provider or supported government integration. Do not scrape the GST portal or store GST portal credentials in the app.
5. Subscription billing provider, plan prices, trial policy, GST treatment, and supported EWB/GST API provider are not specified. Keep these configurable and confirm them before production.

## Shared usability and trust rules

- Design for one-handed use, large touch targets (at least 44 pt), plain language, and accessible contrast.
- Keep advanced/optional fields behind clearly labeled expandable sections; never hide legally required fields.
- Preserve drafts automatically and clearly distinguish **Draft**, **Ready to generate**, **Submitted**, **Generated**, **Failed**, **Expired**, and **Verification unavailable**.
- Explain why a field is required and show validation next to the field.
- Never report a bill as generated until the provider confirms success and returns an EWB number.
- Confirm before consequential actions such as cancel/void, delete, or submitting a bill.
- Display a useful offline/read-only state; queue drafts, but never queue a government submission for silent later execution.
