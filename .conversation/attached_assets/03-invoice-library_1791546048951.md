# Screen 03 — Invoices

## Purpose

Give vendors one straightforward place to find drafts and issued sales invoices, start from one of six reusable layouts, and continue to EWB generation.

## Layout

- Search by invoice number, buyer, GSTIN, or date.
- Filter chips: All, Draft, Ready, EWB generated; date range and GSTIN filters in a filter sheet.
- **New invoice** button and **Import PDF** shortcut.
- Six starter template cards with preview, a short description, and **Use template**. Templates are layouts/default field arrangements, not prefilled legal data.
- Invoice rows/cards: invoice number, buyer, invoice date, total, status, and linked EWB status/number when present.
- Empty state and pagination/infinite scroll.

## Behavior

- Choosing a template opens the editor with that layout and blank business/transaction values.
- Saving creates a draft; editing an invoice preserves the prior version/audit metadata.
- Tapping a row opens the invoice editor or read-only invoice summary, depending on state.
- Do not duplicate invoice numbers silently. Warn on a collision within the issuing GSTIN and let the user resolve it.
- Invoice deletion is allowed only for unsubmitted drafts and requires confirmation. Retain an audit record if policy requires it.

## Data and indexes

- Collection: `businesses/{businessId}/invoices/{invoiceId}`.
- Query by `updatedAt` descending; add composite indexes for `status + updatedAt`, `sellerGstin + invoiceDate`, and status/date filters as needed.
- Store template ID/version on the invoice so later template changes do not rewrite old invoices.

## Acceptance checks

- Template selection never overwrites user-entered details.
- Search and filters work together and remain scoped to the selected business.
- A generated EWB is linked to its source invoice and navigable from the row.
