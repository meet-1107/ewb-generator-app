# Screen 10 — Subscription and usage

## Purpose

Explain plan limits and billing state clearly, and let a business owner manage a subscription without blocking access to their own historic records.

## Layout

- Current plan, renewal date/state, and billing owner.
- Usage meter for the actual plan metric (for example EWB generation count); exact metric and period must be configured before launch.
- Plan comparison with included limits/features, price, billing cadence, and taxes clearly disclosed.
- **Upgrade**, **Manage subscription**, and restore/purchase status actions based on billing platform.
- Trial, payment failure, cancelled, expired, and grace-period states.

## Behavior

- Subscription access decisions are enforced by a trusted backend, not by hiding buttons in the client.
- Never submit a paid subscription or charge without the platform/provider purchase confirmation flow.
- A subscription lapse must not silently delete invoices, EWBs, or user files. State read-only/export behavior and retention policy.
- Do not hard-code a price, trial, cancellation term, or promise until product/billing decisions are approved.
- If both app-store subscriptions and web billing are considered, select a policy compatible with Apple/Google rules and the target markets before implementation.

## Data

- Persist provider customer/subscription IDs only server-side where feasible; expose a minimal entitlement snapshot to the mobile app.
- Webhook event handling must be authenticated, idempotent, logged, and reconciled with provider state.
- Never store payment card details in Firebase.
