# Screen 08 — E-way bills

## Purpose

Provide a searchable archive of all generated bills, including active, expired, cancelled, and provider-verified records.

## Layout

- Search by EWB number, invoice number, buyer, or vehicle.
- Status tabs: All, Active, Expired, Cancelled, Verification needed.
- Filters: date range, GSTIN, buyer, transporter, and vehicle.
- Row/card: EWB number, invoice number, buyer, generated/expiry dates, status, and last verification timestamp.
- Empty/no-results state and paginated results.

## Behavior

- Tap a record to open EWB detail.
- “Verified” is a result supplied by the provider or authoritative source and is shown with source and time; it is not a manually selectable status.
- Expired bills remain searchable and retain their associated invoice/document.
- Refresh individual bill status on demand; avoid an unbounded background poll.
- Show cached data when offline and make the stale timestamp visible.

## Data and indexes

- Collection: `businesses/{businessId}/ewbs/{ewbId}`.
- Support indexed filters by status + `generatedAt`, seller GSTIN + `generatedAt`, and invoice ID.
- Archive visibility is governed by business membership and retention rules.
